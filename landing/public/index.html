FAQ

Is this against Facebook's terms?

Bullseye accesses public listings without authentication, similar to how a browser does. We do not log into your Facebook account.

Will my Facebook account get banned?

Bullseye does not log into your Facebook account, so your account cannot be banned through this tool.

Why is it cheaper than alternatives?

Our architecture runs scraping on your machine, so we don't pay for proxy infrastructure. That's why we can charge $9.99 instead of $16-352/month.

Mac support?

Coming soon. Windows-only at launch — leave your email below to get notified.

How accurate are the scores?

Each score is backed by real eBay sale comps with confidence intervals. Parts/accessories are filtered out automatically. See our blog post for the full methodology.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime. Pro includes a 7-day free trial (no card required) and a 14-day money-back guarantee after that.

What data do you collect?

Your email, your saved watches (so you can restore them on a new device), and anonymized usage analytics (opt-out in settings). Listings stay on your machine.
