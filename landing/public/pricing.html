Billing questions

Do I need a credit card to start the trial?

No. The 14-day Pro trial is completely card-free — sign in with Google and you're in. We only ask for a card if you decide to keep Pro after the trial ends.

How do I cancel?

One click in the Stripe customer portal — there's a "Manage subscription" link in the desktop app and in every billing email we send you. Cancel any time before your next renewal and you won't be charged again.

What's the refund policy?

Full refund within 14 days of your first paid charge — that's 14 days after the trial ends, so effectively 21 days from sign-up to decide. Email hello@getbullseye.app and you'll have it back the same day.

Can I switch between monthly and yearly?

Yes, in the customer portal. Stripe pro-rates the difference automatically.

What happens to my watches if I downgrade?

Nothing's deleted. Free tier shows you the most recent 3 — the rest stay paused on your machine, ready to re-enable if you upgrade again.
