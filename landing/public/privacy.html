Privacy Policy

Last updated: pending launch · Governing law: British Columbia, Canada

Heads up. This is a plain-English summary of how Bullseye handles data. The full legally-binding privacy policy will be generated through Termly before public launch and pasted here, replacing this page. If anything below differs from the final Termly document, the Termly document controls.

What we collect

  • Your email address, via Google OAuth, so we can attach your subscription to an account and send you alerts and receipts.
  • Your watches (keyword, location, radius, filters), so they sync across machines if you reinstall.
  • Anonymized telemetry — error reports, feature-usage counters. You can turn this off in settings.
  • Billing metadata — Stripe customer ID, subscription status, period end. We never see your full card number.

What we don't collect

  • Your Facebook login. Bullseye never authenticates with Facebook at all — there's nothing to take.
  • The listings you look at. Marketplace results stay on your machine; our cloud only sees aggregate stats like "5 watches polled today".
  • Your card number. Stripe handles that end-to-end.
  • Anything we'd consider sellable. We don't have an ad business, and we won't.

Third parties we share data with

  • Stripe — billing.
  • Supabase — our database, auth, and serverless functions.
  • Resend — outbound email.
  • Sentry — crash reports (opt-out in settings).
  • Google — OAuth sign-in only, scopes limited to email and profile.
  • eBay — comp lookups via their developer API. Only the search query goes; never your account info.

Your rights

Under PIPEDA (Canada), GDPR (EU), and CCPA (California), you can ask us at any time to access, export, or delete your data. Email hello@bullseye.app from the address tied to your account and we'll action it within 30 days. Exports are JSON; deletion is irreversible.

Data retention

  • Telemetry events: 90 days, then aggregated and the raw rows dropped.
  • Email send log: 1 year (we need this for deliverability disputes).
  • Account data: until you delete the account, or 12 months after subscription cancellation, whichever is later.

Contact

Privacy questions: hello@bullseye.app. We'll usually answer the same day.
